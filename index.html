      How to play
      • Everyone gets a secret role. Tap your card to look, and keep it to yourself.
      • Night: the Werewolves pick someone to kill. The Seer learns whether one player is a werewolf. The Doctor protects one player (not the same one two nights running). Villagers pick who they suspect, so nobody can tell who's busy.
      • Day: the village learns who died and what they were. Talk it out, then vote. The player with the most votes is cast out; a tie or a win for "skip" means nobody.
      • The village wins when every werewolf is dead. The werewolves win once they match the villagers in number.
      • Optional roles the host can switch on: the Hunter fires one last shot when they die, the Jester wins alone if the village votes them out, and the Minion knows the wolves and wins with them, while the Seer sees them as human. Each one replaces a villager.
      • Dead players watch in silence. No hints to the living!
